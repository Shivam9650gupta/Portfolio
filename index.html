✕

  • On-time / late submission tracking logged per person
  • Slack alert to reporting manager when submission is late or missed
Outcomes
  • 100% visibility into submission compliance
  • Fully automated distribution — zero manual forwarding
  • Late submissions surfaced immediately to management

  • Instant confirmation sent to submitter on creation
  • Slack notification on assignment with assignee details
  • Slack notification when due date is set or changed
  • Slack notification when task is marked complete
Outcomes
  • Zero requests lost or untracked
  • Submitters always informed of status without chasing
  • Full audit trail in project management tool
